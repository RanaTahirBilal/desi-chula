Frequently asked questions

Can I place an order online right now?+

Not yet. This is a preview of the new Desi Chula site. You can build a cart and look around, but checkout runs in demo mode: no order is sent and no payment is taken. Online ordering opens once pickup, delivery and payment are set up.

Is the food halal?+

Yes. Desi Chula is a halal food business. Details about meat suppliers will be listed here once they are confirmed.

Where do you serve?+

Desi Chula plans to serve customers across Long Island, New York. The exact towns and any delivery radius will be listed here once they are confirmed.

Do you offer pickup or delivery?+

Pickup and delivery details, including times and any fees, will be published here before ordering opens.

How far ahead should I book catering?+

The earlier the better. Catering lead times are being finalized. Send an inquiry with your event date and the team will confirm what is possible. An inquiry is not a confirmed booking.

How many people does a tray feed?+

As a guide: small trays serve about 5 to 7 people, medium about 8 to 10 and large about 12 to 15. Actual servings depend on the dish, portion sizes and what else is on the table.

Are portion sizes weights or volumes?+

Container sizes like 24 fl oz measure volume, not weight. Rice, curry and desserts have different densities, so a container's food weight varies by dish. For reference: 8 fl oz is about 237 mL, 16 fl oz about 473 mL, 24 fl oz about 710 mL and 32 fl oz about 946 mL.

What about allergies?+

The menu includes dishes made with dairy, wheat and nuts. Full ingredient and allergen details for each dish are being finalized. If you have a food allergy, please contact us before ordering.

What is panjiri?+

Panjiri is a traditional Punjabi sweet made from flour roasted in ghee with sugar, often mixed with nuts, dried fruit or gond (edible gum). It keeps well and is often shared as a gift.
