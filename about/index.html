Food that is meant to be passed around

Desi Chula is about the meal everyone remembers: the full table.

In a Desi home, the best meals are the crowded ones. A pot of biryani in the middle of the table, rotis wrapped in a cloth, someone asking for more raita. Desi Chula is built around that kind of meal.

The menu sticks to the dishes people grow up with: karahi, nihari, haleem, daal chawal, halwa puri. Portions are listed plainly, family meals come with the sides sorted, and trays are sized for a crowd.

Chula is the clay stove at the heart of a traditional kitchen. The name is a reminder of where these recipes come from, and of the hospitality that goes with them.

What we care about

Food for the whole table

Family meals and trays are built to be shared, with the sides included so nothing is missing.

Familiar dishes, done properly

Biryani, karahi, nihari and haleem: the dishes people know by heart, made the way they are meant to taste.

Clear about what you get

Every dish lists its portion and container size, so ordering for four or forty is straightforward.

Hospitality first

In a Desi home, guests are fed well. That is the spirit behind the menu.
