Food that is meant to be passed around

Mom’s cooking, made with love for your dawat and every occasion.

In a Pakistani home, the best meals are the crowded ones. The dastarkhwan is spread, a pot of biryani goes in the middle, rotis come wrapped in a cloth, and someone always asks for more raita. Desi Chula is built around that kind of meal.

Every dish is cooked by Mom, with love. Karahi, nihari, haleem, daal chawal, halwa puri: the dishes people grow up with, made with a mother's touch for your dawat, your Eid table and every occasion worth celebrating. Ammi ke haath ka khana, shared with Long Island.

Portions are listed plainly, family meals come with the sides sorted, and trays are sized for a crowd.

Chula is the clay stove at the heart of a traditional Pakistani kitchen. The name is a reminder of where these recipes come from, and of the mehmaan nawazi, the hospitality, that goes with them. Everything on the menu is halal.

What we care about

Food for the whole dastarkhwan

Family meals and trays are built for the shared spread, with the sides included so nothing is missing.

A mother's touch

Every dish is cooked by Mom, with love: biryani, karahi, nihari and haleem, made with the care of a meal for her own family.

Clear about what you get

Every dish lists its portion and container size, so ordering for four or forty is straightforward.

Mehmaan nawazi

In Pakistani homes a guest is a blessing, and no one leaves hungry. That spirit of hospitality is behind the menu.
